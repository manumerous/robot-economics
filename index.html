Autonomous deployment cost analysis

Operation Cost vs Autonomy Ratio

Robot operation cost is calculated as: (Fixed Costs + Operator Cost) ÷ Productivity Rate. Fixed costs combine hardware amortization (production cost spread over the amortization period) and hourly maintenance costs (annual maintenance percentage converted to hourly rate). Operator cost is the hourly operator wage divided by the autonomy ratio (robots per operator). The sum is then adjusted for productivity rate to account for the robots being slower at doing the tasks as the human (inlcudes charging, reduced manipulation speed, ect.).
